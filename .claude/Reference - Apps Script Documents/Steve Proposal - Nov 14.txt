Lease Quoting Tool for Partners

Steve,											November 14, 2025
I have spent at least 25 hours researching the best possible approach for the lease quoting tool.   I have explored this as thoroughly as I think necessary and fed specs and desired outcomes into different AI models multiple times.  My initial estimate when we spoke on the phone about this was anywhere from $15,000 to over $30,000 subject to further investigation.  
Going through this process has reminded me of the many times we talked with Kevin Moshayedi about development costs for EPIK.  It was so easy for costs to get out of control, for scope and deliverables aligned to goals to become vague.  I experienced something similar at Granite when I was directing the work of one of Kevin’s developers when we were working on the Epik Install Wizard.  Custom software can be extremely difficult to keep within budget, deliver on time and with desired functions.  And I know that managing developers is a challenge (just ask Kevan) because they tend to have a mind of their own and interpret things how they interpret them which causes reworks, delay and added cost.  
The True Cost of Custom Development.  Estimates for this project.
* Upfront development: $15,000-30,000.  Possibly more.
* Hosting and infrastructure: $50-200/month ongoing
* SSL certificates and domain management: $100-300/year
* Regular security updates and patches: $1,000-2,000/year
* Bug fixes and maintenance: $1,500-3,000/year
* Making changes when your needs evolve: $125-150/hour
The Quality and Risk Problems:
* Developer quality is a gamble - you often do not know what you are getting until thousands of dollars spent.
* Many developers underbid to win the job, then cut corners or deliver poor code
* If the original developer disappears, finding someone to maintain their code is expensive and difficult
* Custom code often has hidden bugs that only surface after launch
* No guarantee the developer understands lease financing or business requirements
* Timeline estimates are frequently wrong 
The Timeline and Complexity Reality:
* Traditional web development requires coordinating multiple systems: frontend, backend, database, hosting, email service
* Each integration point is an opportunity for things to break and this is where developers are really needed
* Testing across browsers, devices, and scenarios can add weeks to the timeline
* Deployment and launch involve technical complexity most business owners do not want to deal with
* After launch, you are responsible for keeping everything running and updated (i.e., paying for it)

Most concerning is you become dependent on the developer. When you need changes in 6 months, you are either stuck paying their rates (often higher for maintenance than development) or trying to find a new developer willing to work on someone else's code (which they typically charge a premium for because they must learn the system first).
After looking at various options I found what is probably the best way to approach this.  It accomplishes everything you need while avoiding every single one of these developer-related problems. No hosting headaches, no developer dependency, no complexity - just a tool that works.  
Recommendation: Google Apps Script Platform
I am proposing we build this using Google Apps Script platform. This is not a compromise or a shortcut - it is a better approach for a tool like this. Here is why:
Infrastructure & Reliability
* Runs on Google servers (free, secure, 99.9% uptime)
* No hosting costs, ever
* No security updates needed - Google handles automatically
* Works on any device with a browser
* Automatic backups and disaster recovery
* Enterprise-grade infrastructure without enterprise costs
Hybrid Approach
I will build the core functionality myself—the forms, business logic, calculations, and data flow. That is where most of the work and value is.  Leveraging AI tools I can develop the underlying code.  For specialized components like PDF generation and email integration, I will bring in specialists for specific tasks rather than hiring an expensive full-time developer for all development.  There are many of these types of developers available for contracting projects online.  I will search for and vet out the right developer depending on the need at the time.  Likely need will be a Google Apps Script certified developer with PDF generation experience.  This gives us custom functionality at a fraction of traditional development costs.
This hybrid approach means you get the best of both worlds: Custom business logic tailored to what we need built on Google’s very reliable platform that Google maintains and secures for free.

Deliverables
Includes:
* Fully functional quoting tool for partner reps
* Shows 36- and 60-month quote comparison for < $100k and > $100k < $500k equipment amounts
* This proposal assumes that most quotes would be in these two price ranges.  
* Adding quoting for 60 and 72-month terms over $500k would add to the project cost.
* Clean customer-facing PDFs (this is one of the most challenging parts of this project)
* All the calculations and financial projections you specified
* Fully editable rate factor table.  Can update rate factors at any time without support
* Multi-location support (1-20 locations per quote)
* Quote email delivery
* Zero hosting or infrastructure costs (forever)
* Extremely low maintenance requirements
* Tool you own forever (low to no subscription costs)
* No single developer dependency since it is built on the Google AppScript platform
* Changes and updates are not nearly as expensive as maintaining custom development work
Doesn’t Include (vs. $30K+ custom app):
* Super polished user interface
* Custom branding and design (aside from logo on PDF)
* Native mobile apps (iOS/Android)
* Advanced features like user accounts, quote history database, CRM integration
This is a business tool built for function, not beauty. The customer-facing PDF will look professional and polished. The rep interface will be clean and straightforward—form-based rather than cutting-edge design. It provides 90% of the value at 30% of the cost while eliminating most headaches.

Scope & Limitations:
This tool is designed specifically for quote generation. It will NOT include:
* Quote history database or CRM functionality
* User accounts with login credentials or user activity tracking
* Customer-facing portal or self-service features
* Integration with other business systems
* Custom branding beyond basic professional design
* Mobile apps (works in mobile browsers, but not native apps)
* Analytics or reporting on quote data
* Quote comparison tools or export to formats other than PDF
* Customer portal for self-service quoting
* Customer digital signature capture, quote acceptance or rejection tracking
* Automated follow-up email reminders, calendaring or scheduling features
* Custom domain name, API integration or offline functionality

Any of the above features may be quoted and added in future phases if needed but would require separate project quotes based on complexity.
