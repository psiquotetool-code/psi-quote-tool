1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
1.1 What This Tool Does
The Lease Quote Tool allows partner reps to:
* Enter details for unlimited locations
* Automatically calculate lease payments for 36 and 60-month terms or 60 and 72-month terms depending on cost of equipment.
* Steve agreed to add 60/72 month quote flexibility on 11/17/2025 email for additional fee as per Proposal.
* Calculate projected utility savings and ROI for each term.  See template for details
* Generate a professional PDF quote for the customer
* Email the PDF to the customer , rep and Steve
* Save a record of quotes generated.
1.2 Success Criteria
* Reps can create accurate quotes in under 10 minutes
* All calculations match Steve's current manual process
* PDFs are professional and customer-ready
* Tool requires minimal training to use
* Admin can see historical quotes and view the pdfs that were generated.  But cannot edit an old quote.  
1.3 Out of Scope (Not in This Version)
* Quote history database
* User accounts/login system
* CRM integration
* Customer self-service portal
* Email automation
* User tracking
* Dashboard
* Export to Excel, etc. other than pdf
* Additional pdf templates & additional user features
* Custom Email Templates
* Quote approval workflow
* Address autocomplete
* eSignature
	





2. User Experience Overview
1. User clicks link to open quote tool
a. We will have two separate URLs (one for Exact Water and one for Tune), but they will use the same code base to calculate.  Branding will be different for each one.
b. Quotes will be generated from same tool.
2. User is presented with a page that has a form on it.
3. User enters the following information at the top of the form (all fields are required)
a. Rep Name
b. Rep Phone
c. Rep Email
d. Customer Company
e. Customer Contact Name
f. Customer Phone
g. Customer Email
4. The tool will automatically capture the following information:
a. Quote Date (set to today)
b. Quote Number (auto generated)
5. User chooses how many locations they want to quote by entering in a number.  Will allow unlimited locations, but they will be required to add information for all of the locations they add.  
6. Based on the number of locations chosen, user enters the following information for each of the locations (all fields are required):
a. Address or Site Name (text field)
b. Utility Provider (User finds this information on their own)  Text field.
c. Monthly Water or Energy Spend from Provider
d. Cost of Equipment Quoted by Provider
e. Estimated Savings Quoted by Provider
f. Number of Electrical Panels for Tune Energy quotes or number of water meters for Exact Water quotes
7. User may add or remove locations before generating the quote.  Give the user flexibility.
8. User may start quote over.  Will be warned that they will lose all information if they proceed.  We are not allowing them to save and come back later so the must have a clear warning that they are about to lose their work.
9. User selects “Generate Quote” button
a. Calculation takes place behind the scenes
b. The equipment amount being financed will automatically determine whether a 36/60 month comparison is taking place or if it should show a 60/72 month comparison.
c. This will then be added to the quote output dynamically.
10. A quote is displayed on the screen utilizing all information provided by the user.
11. The quote displayed on the screen (not pdf yet) shows the following information
a. The top part of the quote will show summary information from step 4 above
b. Summary “card” that shows overall data summarized for all locations
i. (Historical Context:  Determined that there will be no per-location cards.  These would compete with the quoting / presentation strategy Steve uses.  If reps want to do multi-location quotes then we will only be providing the totals for all locations and not showing a per-location breakout.  This is primarily because multiple locations could cause another tier for rate factor selection to be required which would conflict with pricing per site.  So, customer can either have multiple single-site quotes in which the site would have its own billing or there can be a single multi-site quote, but corporate billing would result.  Billing has nothing to do with this project, but gives context to why Steve wanted to do it this way.)

12. User will be able to:
a. Go back to prior screen if they need to add another location
b. Go back to prior screen if they need to remove a location
c. Go back to prior screen if they need to edit any information on a quote 
13. After user is satisfied with quote results (i..e, no additional changes to location information), user can select “Generate PDF Preview” 
Steve and Matt discussed perhaps having the option of exporting to other formats like Excel or PDF, but it was finally determined that we would not do this and Steve will train his reps to 1) save the quote on the screen and proof-read carefully before finishing; and 2) know that a new quote is a quick task.
14. After user previews generated pdf, they can click button “Send Quote to Customer”.  This option will allow the user:
a. To see the boilerplate email to be sent to the customer
b. Edit the email boilerplate to make more personal if needed (i.e., such as if the customer is getting another quote from the rep)
c. Automatically attach the quote to the outgoing customer email as an attachment
d. Automatically cc the user/rep generating the quote to their own email address (they will also get the attachment)
e. Automatically send the quote to Steve Olsen along with customer information.  Steve asked that this be a separate email to him (i.e., not a cc or bcc on the email to the customer)
15. After quote is emailed to customer, rep and Steve:
a. Rep may download the pdf
b. Generate another quote
c. Leave the quote tool (finished quoting for now)

Admin
* Steve will have the ability to see the rate factor table and update rate factors in the future.  All rate factor changes will prospectively impact all future quotes.  There will not be ability to have more than one active rate table.

4.3 Tier Determination Rules
** Confirm how rates are applied to a quote when there are multiple sites being quoted:
Question / ItemNotes / ConfirmationSteve and I discussed extensively.  This will work as follows:  If customer wants a multi-site quote the user will add multiple locations and the aggregated equipment cost and savings will determine the rate factor, not each individual site.  This will prevent the possibility of having a single quote that compares rental payments for individual sites and an aggregated lease payment that could have a different rate factor.  OROption 2: The total is calculated for the entire portfolio of sites on the quote and rate factor is applied to the total, and there is one total for all sites.

Option 2 Example:
Location 1: $60,000 
Location 2: $55,000 
Total Equipment: $115,000 * .03274 (T2 Factor) 
Total Lease Payment = $3,765.10

Difference of $1.15
This option 2 is how it will always be calculated whether the quote is for one site or many sitesHow are # of panels included—if at all—in lease payment calculation?Steve confirmed that the number of panels has nothing to do with our calculation in this quote tool.
