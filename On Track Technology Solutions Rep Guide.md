## How to Create a Quote

### Step 1: Enter Customer Information

1. Open the **Quote Generator** link above
2. Fill in the customer details:
   - **Company Name** - The customer's business name
   - **Customer Contact** - Name of your point of contact
   - **Customer Email** - Where the quote will be sent
   - **Customer Phone** - 10-digit phone number
3. Fill in your rep information:
   - **Rep Name** - Your name
   - **Rep Email** - Your email (you'll be CC'd on the quote)
   - **Rep Phone** - Your 10-digit phone number
4. Click **NEXT: Add and Edit Locations for Quote**

### Step 2: Add Location Details

For each customer location, enter:

| Field | Description | Valid Range |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Address/Site Name** | Location identifier | Required |
| **Utility Provider** | Water utility company | Required |
| **Monthly Spend** | Customer's current monthly water bill | $1 - $250,000 |
| **Equipment Cost** | Cost of meters to be installed | $1 - $1,000,000 |
| **Savings %** | Expected water savings percentage | 1% - 50% |
| **# Meters** | Number of meters at this location | 1 - 2,000 |

**Adding Multiple Locations:**
- Click **+ Add Location** to add more rows (up to 33 locations per quote)
- Click the **X** button on any row to remove it

When finished, click **NEXT: Generate Quote**

### Step 3: Review the Quote

The system will calculate and display:
- **Quote Highlights** - Total equipment cost, meters, and projected savings
- **Lease Options** - Monthly payments and ROI for different term lengths
- **Location Details** - Breakdown by location

**Available lease terms depend on total equipment cost:**

| Total Equipment Cost | Available Terms |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| Under $100,000 | 36-month and 60-month |
| $100,000 - $499,999 | 36-month and 60-month |
| $500,000+ | 60-month and 72-month |

### Step 4: Generate PDF and Send Email

1. Click **Generate PDF** to create the quote document
2. Click **Review & Send Email** to open the email preview
3. Review the email content (you can edit if needed)
4. Click **Send Email**

The quote will be emailed to:
- **Customer** - At the email address you entered
- **You (CC)** - At your rep email address
- **Administrator** - For record keeping

### Step 5: After Sending

You'll see options to:
- **Modify the quote** - Make changes and generate a new quote (gets a new quote number)
- **Download PDF** - Save a copy to your computer
- **Generate New Quote** - Start fresh with a new customer
- **Exit Quote Tool** - Close the application

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Double-check email addresses** before sending - quotes cannot be unsent
- **Save the PDF** to your computer as a backup
- **Use accurate savings percentages** - this affects all ROI calculations
- **Review the quote summary** on Screen 3 before generating the PDF

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Page won't load | Refresh the browser or try a different browser (Chrome recommended) |
| Validation error | Check that all fields meet the valid ranges listed above |
| Email not received | Check spam folder; verify email address was entered correctly |
| PDF won't download | Try right-clicking the link and selecting "Save link as..." |
